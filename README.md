# For Mariyam Fatima 💜 | Hugs, Kisses & Sweet Treats

A custom, cute interactive webpage dedicated to Mariyam Fatima, designed in her favorite light and dark purple shades, filled with kittens, pastries, ice creams, hugs, and kisses.

## 🌟 Features Included

1. **Purr-fect Hero Greeting & Interactive Kitten Mascot:**
   - Soft lavender & deep purple cloudscape.
   - Interactive SVG Kitten that purrs, winks, squints its eyes happily, and speaks sweet praise when petted/clicked.
2. **Hugs & Kisses Express:**
   - **Send Warm Hug:** Animated hug & kitten counter with heart bursts.
   - **Blow Sweet Kisses:** Kisses dispenser with kiss emoji trails and sparkle sounds.
   - **Mega Love Cuddle Attack:** Triggers a screen-wide love shower.
   - Saves hug & kiss totals automatically in local storage!
3. **💌 Interactive Wax-Sealed Love Letter:**
   - Realistic 3D envelope with a purple heart seal.
   - Clicking open reveals a heartfelt letter written for Mariyam Fatima.
4. **🧁 Mariyam's Sweet Cafe & Ice Cream Parlour 🍨:**
   - Six interactive treats (Lavender Velvet Cupcake, Purple Berry Gelato, Golden Butter Croissant, Berry Love Shortcake, Taro Cloud Boba, Violet Macaron Stack).
   - "Serve Mariyam" interactive modal serving each treat with an adorable personalized note.
5. **🔮 Jar of 100 Sweet Reasons:**
   - Shaking magic jar that pulls out 20+ personalized reasons why Mariyam Fatima is deeply loved.
6. **🐾 Kitten Playground / Sanctuary:**
   - Meet Mochi, Boba, Lavender, and Cupcake! Each kitten has its own personality, treats, and unique cuddly reactions.
7. **📸 Polaroid Memory Scrapbook:**
   - Styled purple polaroids taped with washi tape, ready for photos or cute illustrations.
8. **🎵 Cozy Music Box & Sound FX:**
   - Built-in Web Audio API synthesizer playing a dreamy music-box melody and cute sound effects (pops, sparkles, purrs) without relying on external MP3s.

## 🎨 How to Put Real Photos in the Polaroids (Optional)

In `index.html`, look for `<div class="polaroid-gallery">`. You can replace the `<div class="polaroid-img-wrapper ...">` blocks with your own image tags:
```html
<img src="your-photo.jpg" alt="Mariyam" style="width: 100%; height: 220px; object-fit: cover; border-radius: 4px;">
```
